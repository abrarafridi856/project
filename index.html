WELCOME TO OUR SPACE

A Destination of Comfort, Flavor & Elegance

Nestled conveniently on Station Road, Sribhumi (near Eye Cure and opposite Vikash Textile), Cinnamon Cafe & Restro is crafted to be your home away from home.

Whether you are catching up over an artisan cinnamon latte, having a relaxed work session, savoring our signature creamy white sauce pasta with friends, or gathering the entire family for a grand birthday feast, we bring you unmatched hospitality at friendly prices (₹200–₹400/person).

Artisanal Coffee & Beverages

Handcrafted espresso, spiced cinnamon specialty drinks, cold frappes, and refreshing mocktails.

Gourmet Multi-Cuisine Dining

From veggie-loaded Italian pastas to sizzling continental platters and delicious quick bites.

Memorable Celebrations

Turnkey kid & adult birthday party arrangements with themes, custom balloon arches, music & catering.
